SpeakGrove Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Türkçe

SpeakGrove ("we") is a language-learning app published by Ahmet Sahin. This policy explains what data the app processes, why, and the choices you have. Contact: citrushelp@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Your learning path, placement level, goals, streak, XP, review cards, lesson history and conversation transcripts are stored on your device. You can use the core app without an account. You can export or delete this data from Settings.

We do not sell personal data, show ads, or use advertising identifiers. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Retention and deletion

Local data stays until you delete it in Settings or uninstall the app. Account data is kept while your account exists. AI chat requests are not stored by us after the reply is returned, apart from short-lived operational logs (up to 30 days).

Delete your account

In the app: open Settings → Account → "Delete account" and confirm. Your account, synced progress, family membership and classes are deleted from our servers immediately.

Without the app: email citrushelp@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, subject "SpeakGrove account deletion". We delete the account, progress and family membership within 30 days and confirm by email. Subscriptions must be cancelled separately in Google Play or the App Store.

Children

SpeakGrove is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and object to processing. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and change the date above.
